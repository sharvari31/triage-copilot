# Change the email address on your account

Go to **Settings > Profile > Email** and enter the new address. We send a confirmation link to the new address and a notice to the old one. The change is complete only after you click the link within 24 hours. If you no longer have access to the old email, support can change it after identity verification. The new email must not already be used by another account.
