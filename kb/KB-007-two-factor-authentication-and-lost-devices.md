# Two-factor authentication and lost devices

Enable 2FA under **Settings > Security**. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app and save the 10 backup codes. If you lose your phone, sign in with a backup code and re-enroll the new device. Without backup codes, support must verify your identity (account email, last invoice number, and approximate signup date) before 2FA can be reset. This takes up to 2 business days for security reasons.
