# Privacy and exporting your data

You can download a copy of your data under **Settings > Privacy > Export data**. The export is prepared within 24 hours and emailed as a ZIP file containing JSON and CSV files. We process personal data according to our privacy policy and applicable laws including the DPDP Act and GDPR. To object to processing or ask a privacy question, email privacy@example.com.
