# Downloading invoices and GST receipts

All invoices are available under **Settings > Billing > History**. Click the invoice number to download a PDF. To add a GSTIN or company name to future invoices, edit **Billing details**; past invoices cannot be changed, but billing support can reissue invoices from the last 90 days with corrected tax details. Invoices are emailed to the billing contact address on the day of payment.
