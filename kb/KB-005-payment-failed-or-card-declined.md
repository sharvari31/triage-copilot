# Payment failed or card declined

A payment can fail because of insufficient funds, an expired card, international transactions being disabled, or a bank-side 3-D Secure / OTP timeout. Retry from **Settings > Billing** after checking with your bank. We retry failed renewals automatically on days 1, 3 and 7. After the third failure the account moves to read-only until payment succeeds; no data is deleted.
