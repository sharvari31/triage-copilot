# Track your order

Once your order ships you receive an email and SMS with the tracking number. You can also see live tracking under **Orders > Track**. Tracking can take up to 24 hours to show the first scan after the label is created. If tracking has not updated for more than 4 days, contact support and we will open an investigation with the courier.
