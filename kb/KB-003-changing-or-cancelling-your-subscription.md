# Changing or cancelling your subscription

Open **Settings > Billing > Plan**. Choose **Change plan** to upgrade or downgrade; upgrades are prorated immediately, downgrades take effect at the end of the current period. Choose **Cancel subscription** to stop renewal. You keep access until the period ends and your data is kept for 90 days. Cancelling does not refund the current period unless it was renewed in the last 7 days, in which case a full refund is issued automatically.
