# When will I get my refund?

Refunds are issued after the returned item passes inspection, usually within 2 business days of reaching our warehouse. The money then takes 5-7 business days to appear on cards, 1-3 days for UPI, and is instant for store credit. Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded to a bank account you add under **Orders > Refund details**. You get an email when the refund is issued.
