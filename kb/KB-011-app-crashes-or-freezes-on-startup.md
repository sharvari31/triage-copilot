# App crashes or freezes on startup

Update to the latest app version from the App Store or Play Store first. If it still crashes, clear the app cache (Android: *Settings > Apps > Storage > Clear cache*; iOS: reinstall the app). Make sure the device has at least 500 MB free storage. If the crash continues, send us the device model, OS version and app version from **Profile > About** so engineering can reproduce it.
