# Delete your account and data

The account owner can delete the account under **Settings > Account > Delete account**. Deletion is scheduled for 14 days later and can be cancelled during that window by signing in. After 14 days all personal data, orders history and files are permanently removed, except invoices we must keep for tax law. Active subscriptions must be cancelled first.
