# Shipping times and delivery areas

Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days in metro cities and 5-8 business days elsewhere in India. Express delivery (1-2 business days) is available in selected pincodes at checkout. Orders placed after 2 PM ship the next business day. Remote areas and the North-East may take additional time. Delivery estimates exclude Sundays and public holidays.
