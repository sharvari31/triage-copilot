# Loyalty points and referral rewards

You earn 1 point for every Rs 100 spent; 100 points equal Rs 50 off. Points appear 14 days after delivery once the return window passes, and expire after 12 months. Refer a friend from **Profile > Refer** and you both get Rs 200 credit after their first order is delivered. Points cannot be exchanged for cash.
