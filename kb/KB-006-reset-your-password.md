# Reset your password

Click **Forgot password** on the login page and enter your account email. The reset link is valid for 30 minutes and can be used once. If the email does not arrive within 5 minutes, check spam and make sure you are using the email the account was created with. Accounts that sign in with Google or Microsoft do not have a password; use the matching *Continue with* button instead.
