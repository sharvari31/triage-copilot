# How to update your payment method

Go to **Settings > Billing > Payment methods** and click **Add card**. Enter the new card details and select **Make default**. The old card can then be removed. Changes apply to the next billing cycle; any invoice already marked *Due* will be retried on the new card within 24 hours. UPI and net banking can be added the same way in India. If the Add card button is greyed out, only the account owner can change billing.
