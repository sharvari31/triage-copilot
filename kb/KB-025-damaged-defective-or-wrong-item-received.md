# Damaged, defective or wrong item received

Report it within 48 hours of delivery from **Orders > Report a problem** and attach photos of the item and the packaging. We offer a free replacement or a full refund, including shipping. You do not need to wait for the original item to be picked up before a replacement ships for orders under the value limit.
