# Account locked after too many login attempts

After 5 failed sign-in attempts the account is locked for 15 minutes to protect it from password guessing. Wait 15 minutes and try again, or reset your password to unlock it immediately. Repeated lockouts from unknown locations trigger a security email; if you did not attempt to sign in, reset your password and enable two-factor authentication.
