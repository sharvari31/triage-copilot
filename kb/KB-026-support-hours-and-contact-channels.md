# Support hours and contact channels

Chat and email support are available 24/7. Phone support runs 9 AM to 9 PM IST, Monday to Saturday. Business plan customers get a dedicated account manager. Typical first response times are 2 minutes on chat and 4 hours on email. For security, support will never ask for your password or full card number.
