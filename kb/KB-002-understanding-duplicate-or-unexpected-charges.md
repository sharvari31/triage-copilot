# Understanding duplicate or unexpected charges

A second charge for the same amount is usually a **pending authorization** from a failed attempt, not a real charge. Banks release these within 5-7 business days. Check **Settings > Billing > History**: only rows marked *Paid* are real charges. If two rows are both *Paid* for the same invoice, contact billing support with the invoice numbers and we will refund the duplicate within 3 business days. Annual plans also show a prorated charge when you upgrade mid-cycle.
